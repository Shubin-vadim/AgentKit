# 1970-01-01-example

**Claim:**
A one-file Python script can parse the sample CSV in under 100ms on this machine.

**Falsify:**
Median of 20 runs ≥ 100ms, or parse fails on the sample.

**Data:**
Present: `fixtures/sample.csv`. Grain: one file, ~50k rows.
Missing: none.

**Method:**
1. Write a one-file parser under `.scratch/parse-csv/`.
2. Time 20 runs on this machine.
3. Kill if median ≥ 100ms or parse fails.

**Decide:**
keep — median < 100ms and parse succeeds.
kill — median ≥ 100ms or parse fails.
unknown — sample missing or timer unusable.

**Did:**
`.scratch/parse-csv/run.py` against `fixtures/sample.csv`.

**Result:**
kill — median 180ms. Need a streaming parser or a smaller extract, not this script in prod.

**Throw away:**
`.scratch/parse-csv/`

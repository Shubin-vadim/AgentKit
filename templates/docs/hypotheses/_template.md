# YYYY-MM-DD-slug

**Claim:**
One sentence. What we believe is true.

**Falsify:**
What observation would kill the claim.

**Data:**
Present: paths, grain, fields used.
Missing: what to collect, source, how much is enough to decide.

**Method:**
Numbered steps. One action each.

**Decide:**
keep — the observation that keeps the claim.
kill — the observation that kills it.
unknown — what would still block a verdict.

**Did:**
Empty until a run. Then what we actually ran or built (paths under `.scratch/`).

**Result:**
Empty until a run. Then keep | kill | unknown.

**Throw away:**
Empty until a run. Then what to delete.
